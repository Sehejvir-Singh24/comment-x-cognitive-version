# Saathi SIH Prototype Demo

Target length: 2 minutes 30 seconds
Format: 1920×1080, MP4, 30 fps

## Voice-over and shot list

### 0:00–0:12 — The problem
**Visual:** Title: “Saathi — AI Cognitive Companion” and subtitle “Daily independence for people living with dementia.”

**Voice-over:** “For a person living with dementia, every phone interaction can become confusing. Families also lack a simple way to support daily routines without taking away independence.”

### 0:12–0:30 — Voice-first launcher
**Visual:** Unlock phone. Show Saathi as default home launcher. Tap Speak to Saathi and ask: “Open WhatsApp.”

**Voice-over:** “Saathi replaces the complex Android home screen with an elderly-friendly, voice-first launcher. A patient can speak naturally, open apps, check medicines, and ask about today’s routine.”

### 0:30–0:48 — Memory Passport
**Visual:** Open Memory Passport. Show family member, favourite place, medicine, and routine cards.

**Voice-over:** “A personal Memory Passport stores family, places, medicines, routines, and meaningful life details. Saathi uses this trusted context to answer personal questions and create relevant cognitive exercises.”

### 0:48–1:12 — North-East Memories
**Visual:** Open North-East Memories. Show landmark or textile card. Play five-second Bihu sound and answer once.

**Voice-over:** “Cognitive exercises use culturally familiar North-East Indian stimuli, including Majuli, Loktak Lake, Kangla Fort, living root bridges, regional textiles, and a Bihu sound-matching activity.”

### 1:12–1:30 — Video recall
**Visual:** Open Watch. Play Bihu excerpt. Show immediate recall question.

**Voice-over:** “Video Recall presents a short local festival clip, checks immediate recall, then asks a delayed question later. The app records accuracy, response time, difficulty, and hint use.”

### 1:30–1:56 — Caretaker portal
**Visual:** Show live portal. Open Cognitive Records. Filter North-East Memories. Scroll to Digital Biomarkers.

**Voice-over:** “With explicit cloud sync, the caretaker portal receives Memory Passport updates, medicines, routines, cognitive results, and remote checkup requests. It summarizes response time, time to first hint, and immediate versus delayed recall.”

### 1:56–2:15 — Architecture and privacy
**Visual:** Architecture title card.

**Voice-over:** “Flutter powers the patient experience. Kotlin handles Android launcher functions and system actions. SQLite keeps the app usable locally. Firebase provides consent-based synchronization, while Gemini supports natural conversation.”

### 2:15–2:30 — Close
**Visual:** Final card: “Saathi — Familiar. Supportive. Independent.”

**Voice-over:** “Saathi turns a phone into a familiar cognitive companion—helping patients stay independent while keeping caregivers informed.”

## Recording rules

- Use landscape output. Phone footage appears inside a portrait phone frame.
- Hide notifications and personal messages.
- Use demo patient data only.
- Keep each tap visible for at least one second.
- Do not call Saathi a medical diagnostic device.
- Say the measures relate to memory, attention, and orientation domains; do not claim a validated MoCA correlation.
